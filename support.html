Support

Straight answers before you install.

Does the app include channels?

No. LibertyVue Player is software only — bring your own playlists or free-to-air streams you are authorised to watch.

When does the free trial start?

On first launch, automatically. Seven days, no card required.

Can one licence be used on several TVs?

No — one licence covers one device (one Device ID).

Which playlist formats are supported?

M3U/M3U8 links and Xtream Codes (server + username + password).

A channel has no sound?

Open it, tap the speaker button to try another audio track. Some providers use codecs your device cannot decode — use the external-player button (VLC) in that case.

The app shows a loading message?

Large providers take a while: live channels appear first, then films and series load automatically with progress numbers. If it stops with an error, check your connection and refresh.
