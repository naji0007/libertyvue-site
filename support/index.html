Help centre

Setup help without the guesswork.

Follow the four steps below. If your playlist does not play, test the connection and contact the provider responsible for that playlist.

⬇

1. Install

Install LibertyVue Player on a supported Android TV or Fire TV device.

⌁

2. Note your codes

Open the app and keep the Device ID and Device Key shown on screen.

≣

3. Add a playlist

Open Manage playlists on this website and add an authorised M3U/M3U8 URL.

↻

4. Refresh

Restart or refresh the TV app to load the updated playlist.
