Setup guides

Step-by-step help for installing the app, choosing M3U or Xtream Codes, and connecting an authorised playlist.

Android TV / Google TV

  1. Install the APK from the download page (or via Downloader).
  2. Open the app, tap +, choose M3U or Xtream Codes.
  3. Enter the details from your provider and save. Your library appears after refresh.

Amazon Fire TV

  1. Install Downloader from the Amazon Appstore.
  2. Enter the download link, install, allow unknown sources.
  3. Add your playlist as above. Use the Fire TV remote: arrows + OK.

M3U or Xtream Codes?

  • M3U / M3U8 — a single playlist link from your provider. Simplest option.
  • Xtream Codes — server URL + username + password. Faster browsing, EPG guide included.

Only use playlists you own or are authorised to watch.

While watching

  • CH + / − or up/down arrows: zap channels without leaving playback.
  • OK: channel list with categories, preview and EPG.
  • Back: return to the library. The speaker button opens another player (VLC) for special codecs.
