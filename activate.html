Activate a device

Every new device receives a 7-day free trial automatically — no card required. When it ends, choose the licence that suits you.

How activation works

  1. Open LibertyVue Player on the TV — note the Device ID at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Contact us with the Device ID and the licence you want.
  3. We activate it — refresh the app and the licence appears (annual shows its expiry date).
